ಚೋಮನ ದುಡಿ (Chomana Dudi)

The Drum of Choma: A Tale of Resilience and Soil

By Dr. K. Shivaram Karanth (ಶಿವರಾಮ ಕಾರಂತ) Jnanpith Laureate

An unyielding monument in Indian literature, Chomana Dudi chronicles the devastating yet poetic life of Choma, an untouchable bonded laborer in pre-independence Coastal Karnataka who harbors an impossible, burning dream: to till a piece of land of his own. Denied legal and customary rights to own land due to rigid caste laws, Choma channels his profound grief, anguish, and transcendent spirit into the rhythmic beats of his handheld folk drum (dudi).

Karanth’s unsparing social realism captures the agrarian rhythms, the colonial plantation pressures, and human dignity under systemic deprivation. A timeless classic later made into the celebrated National Award-winning film by B.V. Karanth, this work stands alongside the finest humanitarian literature of the 20th century.
